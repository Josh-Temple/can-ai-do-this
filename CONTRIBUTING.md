# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially real-world questions, official-source corrections, and reproducible capability reports.

## Good contributions

A useful contribution answers one of these needs:

- "People keep asking whether AI can do this."
- "The current answer is stale or wrong."
- "The official documentation changed."
- "I reproduced this capability in a specific environment."
- "This community discussion reveals an important edge case."

## Before submitting a capability claim

Please include:

- the task;
- product;
- plan if known;
- platform;
- region if relevant;
- date checked;
- source URL;
- evidence state;
- limitations or prerequisites.

Do not mark a result VERIFIED unless it was actually reproduced.

## Community links

Links to Reddit and other public discussions are welcome when they demonstrate demand, confusion, edge cases, or user-reported behavior.

Please do not copy large portions of posts or comments into this repository. Summarize in your own words and link to the original discussion.

## Privacy

Do not submit:
- private conversations;
- personal email content;
- API keys, tokens, or credentials;
- unnecessary personal information;
- evidence that you do not have permission to share.

Use synthetic examples whenever possible.
